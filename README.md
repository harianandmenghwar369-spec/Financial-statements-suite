A professional, fully integrated **Three-Statement Financial Model** built entirely in Microsoft Excel. This model serves as a rigorous accounting framework, dynamically linking the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement into a cohesive, formula-driven ecosystem. Designed for corporate evaluation, small business accounting, and stakeholder reporting, it ensures flawless data integrity across all sheets.

## 🚀 Core Functionalities

* **Dynamic Performance Tracking:** Comprehensive Profit & Loss matrix calculating absolute margins across variable operational frequencies (monthly and annual views).
* **Automated Accounting Reconciliation:** Balance Sheet module engineered to maintain total accounting equilibrium ($$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$$) automatically via dynamic income links.
* **Cash Flow Re-routing:** Segregates liquidity tracking into operational, investment, and financial activities to map out net liquidity positioning.
* **Centralized Assumptions Engine:** Decoupled input architecture allowing users to change baseline business metrics in one dedicated sheet to cascade updates everywhere.
* **Presentation-Ready Distribution:** Meticulously formatted with explicit print-area controls to deliver clean, exportable corporate layouts out of the box.

## 📂 Architecture & Sheet Breakdown

The accounting engine is structurally divided into interactive reporting modules:

### 1. Income Statement (Profit & Loss)
* **Database Target:** Monitors operational efficiency margins.
* **Key Components:** Computes Top-line Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Profit, Operating Expenditures (OpEx), EBITDA, and bottom-line Net Profit.

### 2. Balance Sheet
* **Database Target:** Tracks financial health, asset reserves, and capital structure.
* **Key Components:** Separates Current/Non-Current Assets from Current/Long-Term Liabilities, linking directly to Retained Earnings to auto-balance without manual intervention.

### 3. Cash Flow Statement
* **Database Target:** Reconciles accounting profit with absolute cash liquidity.
* **Key Components:** Generates line-item visibility across Operating, Investing, and Financing flows. The closing balance hooks directly into the Balance Sheet cash account using a strict reconciliation loop:
    $$\text{Ending Cash Balance} = \text{Beginning Cash Balance} \pm \text{Net Cash Flow Change}$$

### 4. Consolidated Control Sheet (User Inputs)
* **Operational Rules:** Explicit cell-isolation tab where core trial balance figures or business variables are entered, completely preserving reporting sheets from accidental text overwrites.
